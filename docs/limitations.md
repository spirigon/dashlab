# Limitations and evidence boundaries

- **Approximate physics.** The study establishes paths in `cube-proxy-v1`, not a complete emulation of Geometry Dash. Optional UI mechanics are outside the measured task.
- **Small fixed representation.** Eight templates, four sections, fixed coordinates within patterns. The compiler/library contributes substantially to success. Random repair is therefore an essential control.
- **Known families.** Twelve new numeric configurations reuse six families and the development library. This is not a held-out-mechanics benchmark.
- **Primary result inconclusive.** The sole primary 4B comparison has brief-cluster p=0.125. The 9B comparison is secondary and exploratory; historical development is not an independent confirmatory test.
- **Random repair remains competitive.** 9B local feedback has 14/24 successes versus 11/24 random local repair; the paired difference does not establish superiority. For 4B, random local repair has more successes than feedback.
- **Shared implicit feedback.** No-text and random local conditions still use a measured target and the same acceptance filter. The treatment tests extra diagnostic text, not all possible feedback versus none.
- **Controller contribution.** Zero lost checks in retained states follows from the common guard. The model still proposed harmful edits. A guard can prevent useful temporary sacrifices.
- **No equal-token-budget claim.** Whole/local proposals differ in size and information. Stopping on success changes proposal counts. Two checkpoints do not isolate a causal parameter-count effect.
- **Quality metrics are limited.** Minimum jumps and geometric Jaccard diversity do not establish enjoyment, human difficulty, musical rhythm, or author-time savings.
- **Selected demos.** M01–M04 are post-outcome examples from the recorded study, not four additional evaluation cases. Live playback uses saved responses.

## Native-game evidence

There is one later completion self-report after M01 and M03 were supplied. The association with these IDs is inferred from conversation context; the IDs were not explicitly enumerated. Mode, attempts, and difficulty were not recorded, and there is no independent recording. The original statement is preserved in [native-playtest.json](../results/native-playtest.json).

This is useful informal demonstration evidence but does not alter the frozen 288-chain results or establish a native-game success rate. Earlier human playtests from other development series are not pooled into the final study.

## Next meaningful evaluation

Predeclare a new experiment on held-out families, retain the same fair random and no-text controls, compare costs explicitly, and systematically playtest exports in the actual game with several participants. Measure mode, attempts, progress, and enjoyment separately from simulator success. A positive result is not assumed in advance.
